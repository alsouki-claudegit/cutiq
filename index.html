Four steps from scan to fade

You still hold the clipper. CutIQ handles the part that takes barbers years to learn: knowing which guard goes where and blending between them.

1

Scan

Run the wand over your head, the same way a dentist scans teeth for a retainer. The screen shows the spots it still needs.

2

Pick the cut

Choose a low, mid or high fade and how long you want the top. Or set the length for each area yourself.

3

Cut

Sides and back first, then the top. The screen tells you where to move next while the guard adjusts on its own.

4

Save it

Your scan and your cut stay on your profile. Next time you go straight to cutting.

If it loses track, the blade stops

The worst home haircut is the one spot you took down to the skin by accident. CutIQ watches where the clipper is the whole time, and if it can't tell, it stops cutting until you put it back where the screen shows.

Three sensors in the clipper

  • An optical motion sensor, like the one under a computer mouse, reads how far you moved.
  • A motion chip, like the one in your phone, reads the angle you're holding it at.
  • A small depth camera matches where you are against your head scan.

Questions

I've never cut hair. Can I really do a fade?

That's who it's built for. You move the clipper where the screen tells you. Picking the guard and blending between lengths is the clipper's job.

Does it work on curly or coily hair?

Yes, with the guard made for it. Pick your hair type at setup and CutIQ recommends the right guard and adjusts the plan.

What happens if I move the wrong way?

The blade stops. It starts again once the clipper is back where it should be.

Can my roommate use it too?

Yes. Each person gets a profile with their own scan, hair type and saved cuts.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. More hairstyles may come later as optional add ons, but everything you need to cut works without paying again.
